# Surface brief — / (campaign home)

**Scope & mode:** Persuade. The gateway landing page of the fall paid-media microsite; also the default landing for brand traffic.

**Audience & job:** Families and couples/friend-groups within a ~1.5h drive, arriving from Meta/Instagram ads on phones. Job: decide in seconds which plan fits their crew and tap out to a real itinerary, winery, or event (outbound clicks are the KPI).

**Action:** Primary — "Pick your crate" (audience choice: couples / families / events). Secondary — live winery roster (Whereabouts widget), dated events, stories, map.

**Proof/content:** Live Whereabouts operators widget (wineries), dated events from data, drive times, real regional claims already in copy. Itinerary URLs and event links are placeholders pending client data.

**Chosen direction:** The Fruit Belt Label — Niagara fruit-crate lithography: concord/leaf/persimmon/goldenrod inks owning whole sections over label stock, double-rule plates, Ultra woodtype + Barlow Condensed caps + Yellowtail flourish, authored litho scene hero, lot stamps. Seed 762c6017 (pick card). Memorable moment: the authored escarpment-orchard litho scene under "Fall, *your way*", and the three-crate audience pick.

**Motion:** stamp-&-spring — sections stamp in once (IntersectionObserver, reduced-motion safe); presses spring (iOS-feel cubic-bezier).

**Constraints:** fall campaign theme binding; tracking JS and data-module hooks preserved; mobile-first, iOS-like behavior; no fabricated listings/claims.

**Unresolved:** real photography for story cards; final itinerary/event URLs; Mailchimp + analytics IDs; whether widget internals accept deeper theming (shadow DOM).
