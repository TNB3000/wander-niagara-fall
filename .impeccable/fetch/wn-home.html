Wanderers Welcome!

Welcome to Wander Niagara, your ultimate guide to the region’s “flip side”—where urban energy meets rural charm, and every season brings something new to explore. Venture beyond Niagara Falls to St. Catharines, a vibrant city filled with craft breweries, a thriving culinary scene, boutique shopping, and live entertainment. From historic downtown streets to waterfront trails along Lake Ontario, St. Catharines is Niagara’s urban playground, perfect for foodies, art lovers, and outdoor enthusiasts alike.

Just minutes away, the Niagara Benchlands await with rolling vineyards, lush orchards, and a UNESCO Niagara Escarpment Biosphere. Home to over 50 award-winning wineries, farm-to-table dining, and scenic hiking and cycling routes, this region is a haven for wine lovers and nature seekers. Whether you’re exploring Ball’s Falls, unwinding on sandy beaches, or discovering hidden waterfalls, the Benchlands offer the perfect mix of adventure and relaxation. Plan your getaway with Wander Niagara and uncover the best-kept secrets of this incredible region.

Adventure Awaits!

There are so many ways to enjoy this region. And every season brings its own special rewards for wanderers. Whether you’re here for a day, a weekend, a week or more, find your ideal mix of urban entertainment and fresh-from-the-fields delights or the natural wonders of the Niagara Escarpment.

Niagara Benchlands

Explore the stunning Niagara Benchlands, a haven for wine enthusiasts and nature lovers alike. With over 50 craft wineries dotting its steep slopes, orchards, and rural villages, this region boasts award-winning wines and breathtaking landscapes. Discover local markets, hidden shops, and rich history all amidst a food lover’s paradise shaped by the unique climate and terroir of the area. Perched on the Niagara Escarpment, the Benchlands offer a wealth of natural wonders, including the renowned Bruce Trail and UNESCO Biosphere.

St. Catharines

Nestled in Ontario’s picturesque Niagara Region, St. Catharines is a vibrant destination that perfectly blends culinary excellence, a thriving craft beverage scene, rich cultural experiences, and abundant outdoor activities. Savor farm-to-table delights at local restaurants, explore renowned wineries and craft breweries, and immerse yourself in the arts at theaters and galleries. Outdoor enthusiasts can enjoy scenic parks, hiking the Bruce Trail, and water sports in the historic waterfront neighbourhood of Port Dalhousie. Whether you’re a foodie, wine lover, culture aficionado, or adventure seeker, St. Catharines offers something for everyone.
