Autumn's arrival brings a whole host of fun and tasty ways to experience harvest season in Niagara. Whether you love the excitement of events and festivals, discovering a new place to sip or dine, or a sunny afternoon setting out on your own path to discover the freshest of autumn flavours, here are just a handful of the many ways to eat and drink your way through Niagara this fall.

## Experience The Excitement of The Niagara Grape & Wine Fall Festival

A must-visit festival for food and wine lovers, the 75th [Niagara Grape & Wine Fall Festival](https://niagarawinefestival.com/grape-and-wine) features local eats, top area wineries, music, and more. Visitors can enjoy exclusive on-site winery experiences with three weekends of [Discovery Pass](https://niagarawinefestival.com/fall-discovery-pass-2026) options, offering passport holders access to delicious wine-and-food pairings at their choice of up to six wineries. The festival concludes with a crashing crescendo from September 24 - 27 when St. Catharines' historic Montebello Park is transformed into a massive food and wine village, complete with 100 Niagara VQA wines, delicious curated snacks by talented local chefs, live music and posh VIP options like wine cabanas with dedicated service from a personal wine butler!

## Set Out On A Fall Winery Road Trip

The Grape & Wine Festival's Discovery Pass program isn't the only way to get out and taste the harvest at Niagara wineries this fall. To find other excellent options worthy of timing a trip around, browse the [Niagara Benchlands Wineries event calendar](https://niagarabenchlandswineries.com/pages/events), which offers autumn adventures that include a [Fall Wine Trail](https://winetrail.trilogen.ca/) event running between five different wineries, a From Beamsville to Burgundy tasting at [Domaine Le Clos Jordanne](https://www.leclosjordanne.com/discover/domaine-le-clos-jordanne/), a Grilled Cheese Extravaganza at [Sue-Ann Staff Estate Winery](https://www.sue-annstaff.com/), a Passport to Pinot event at [Cave Spring Vineyard](https://cavespring.ca/), and so much more!

## Spend A Morning At the Markets

Experience Niagara's farmers markets, roadside stands, and pick-your-own farms when the fall harvest is at its peak. Leave plenty of space in your trunk to stock up on fresh produce, scratch-baked treats, preserves, meats, and more at the [St. Catharines Farmers Market](https://www.stcatharines.ca/arts-culture-and-events/farmers-market/), along the [CultivATE Taste Trail](https://www.niagarabenchlands.ca/cultivate), and at other friendly local farms like [Overholt Orchards Farm Market](https://free-5233535.webadorsite.com/).

## Meet Your Favourite Restaurant or Winery's Savoury Sibling

The soaring popularity of several Niagara restaurants has inspired delicious new expansions. If you love the MICHELIN-starred [Restaurant Pearl Morissette](https://restaurantpearlmorissette.com/), be sure to stop in for fresh baking and coffee bar options at [RPM Bakehouse](https://rpmbakehouse.com/). If the MICHELIN-recommended [Fat Rabbit](https://fat-rabbit.ca/) wowed you, a meal at [Les Incompetents](https://www.barlesincompetents.ca/) is a must! [Odd Bar's](https://oddbird.ca/) pizza and wings are equally as impressive as [Odd Bird's](https://oddbird.ca/) tasty blackboard menu. The award-winning wines at [Henry of Pelham Family Estate](https://henryofpelham.com/) and [13th Street Winery](https://13thstreetwinery.com/) taste even better when paired with the fabulous patio fare served at their [Short Hills Kitchen](https://henryofpelham.com/short-hills-kitchen/) and [The Vineyard Kitchen Bistro](https://13thstreetwinery.com/casual-dining/). Fans of [The Valley Restaurant's](https://www.valleyrestaurant.net/) delicious old-school Italian options will love the vibe at the newly opened Café Pamela<!-- LINK TBD — ask client for the correct Café Pamela URL -->, which is just a short stroll from incredible golden-hour views at [Sunset Beach](https://lovestc.ca/business/sunset-beach-boat-launch/).

## Try Niagara's Tastiest New Collabs & Off-The-Radar Gems

Niagara always has a few tasty tricks up its sleeve even for those who think they already know its food scene well! [The Cargo Kitchen](https://www.uppercanadacheese.com/) is a creative new culinary collaboration between [Upper Canada Cheese Company](https://www.uppercanadacheese.com/) and [Stick Shift Robata](https://www.instagram.com/stickshiftrobata/) celebrating Japanese culinary craftsmanship, Niagara agriculture, and award-winning local cheese with a tempting array of options that includes Wagyu Gravy Poutine and shaved ice treats. The Patio at [Westcott Vineyards](https://www.westcottvineyards.com/) is also doing some really delicious things with its wood-fired stone oven, hardwood grill, and chef's garden. [The Tangerine Tortoise](https://tangerinetortoise.ca/), a tiny restaurant housed in a former appliance shop in a historic St. Catharines neighbourhood, bakes up next-level pizzas thanks to its three-day dough process, which uses three forms of fermentation. [Pique-Nique](https://www.piqueniquecafe.ca/), located in the same neighbourhood as Tangerine Tortoise, is also well worth a visit for French-inspired pastries, breakfast and lunch dishes, and all the espresso-based drinks! [The Good Earth Food & Wine Co.](https://goodearthfoodandwine.com/) is by no means new on the Niagara food scene, but is well worth checking out if it hasn't yet arrived on your radar.

## Travel Back In Time at Lincoln Museum & Cultural Centre's Harvest Pioneer Day

Follow the scent of freshly made apple fritters through historic [Jordan Village](https://www.jordanvillage.ca/) on September 19 to [Lincoln Museum & Cultural Centre's](https://lincolnmuseum.ca/) 60th annual Harvest Pioneer Day. In addition to tasty fritters, visitors can enjoy a delicious assortment of food truck fare as they browse artisan vendors' wares, watch historical demonstrations, listen to live music, and more. While you're in the neighbourhood, it's also a great opportunity to stop in for lunch at [Inn on the Twenty](https://www.vintage-hotels.com/inn-on-the-twenty/), and shop for special finds in Jordan Village's interesting assortment of boutiques and galleries.

## Sip & Snack To The Sounds of the Cicada Music & Arts Festival

Folks who prefer their craft sips and snacks paired with live Canadian music won't want to miss October's [Cicada Music & Arts Festival](https://www.cicadafestival.ca/) in Port Dalhousie. An array of incredible Canadian talent, including Metric, Bahamas, and Hawksley Workman, will perform from two separate stages on Henley Island on October 2 and 3. Onsite beverage vendors include [Dillon's Small Batch Distillery](https://www.dillons.ca/visit-us/), offering perfect botanical gin pairings for crisp fall days.

## Caesar It Up At The Sip Niagara Festival

If sipping a Caesar while enjoying a gorgeous Lake Ontario view is your idea of a perfect way to spend an October afternoon, add the [Sip Niagara Food & Beverage Festival](https://www.sipniagara.com/) to your fall to-do list! In addition to a massive array of savoury snacks, Niagara wineries, crafty cocktails, refreshing beers, ciders, and ready-to-drink beverage options, the Festival boasts the world's largest Caesar bar, complete with over 200 unique ingredients.

## Taste A Huge Assortment of Foodie Fare at the Ball's Falls Thanksgiving Festival

Fill your long weekend with one of Niagara's most beloved fall traditions with a visit to the [Ball's Falls Thanksgiving Festival](https://thanksgivingfestival.ca/), with over 55 fabulous food and beverage vendors! In addition to selling fresh fall flavours to enjoy right on the spot, several vendors offer packaged items that are a wonderful way to get a head start on holiday shopping for your favourite foodies!
