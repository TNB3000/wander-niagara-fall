Fall is the perfect time for a fun-filled family getaway to Niagara. Whether you've got little ones who can't yet walk, toddlers who are eager to start exploring, grade schoolers overflowing with enthusiasm to learn something new, or older kids you're trying to entice away from their screens, Niagara's got age-appropriate options that will make the trip an absolute blast for everyone!

Here are some of the best ways to spend a fall day with the family in Niagara.

## Baby-Friendly Ways To Spend A Fall Day

Paved trails make autumn exploring easy for families with little ones still in the stroller stage. [The Welland Canal Trail](https://ontariobiketrails.com/item/welland-canal-park-trail/) is an excellent option, with plenty of sights for parents to take in along the way. Stop for a mid-walk visit and picnic at the [St. Catharines Museum and Welland Canals Centre](https://www.stcatharines.ca/arts-culture-and-events/st-catharines-museum-and-welland-canals-centre/). If you prefer to bring baby along in a sling or pack, Niagara offers several lovely walking trails that aren't too rugged or steep. Grab an iced coffee at [Bright Pours Cafe](https://www.brightpourscafe.ca/) and just stroll across the street to hop on the tree-lined [Merritt Trail](https://www.ontariotrails.on.ca/trail/merritt-trail), which follows the historic path of the first, second, and third Welland Canals and Twelve Mile Creek. [Calamus Estate Winery](https://calamuswines.com/) has beautiful [walking trails](https://calamuswines.com/hiking-trails/) that visitors are encouraged to explore.

## Toddler-Approved Autumn Activities

A ride on the historic [Lakeside Park Carousel](https://lovestc.ca/business/lakeside-park-carousel/) is a rite of passage for kids in Niagara! Get there for your five-cent ride before things shut down for the season on Thanksgiving Monday. While you're nearby, head over to [Happy Rolph's Animal Farm](https://lovestc.ca/business/happy-rolphs-animal-farm/), also open through Thanksgiving weekend. Little ones will love interacting with friendly farm animals and feeding the waterfowl paddling happily in the onsite ponds.

Introducing toddlers to the fresh flavours of harvest season in Niagara is a fun activity to schedule around snacktime! Visit the [St. Catharines farmers market](https://www.stcatharines.ca/arts-culture-and-events/farmers-market/) on Thursdays and Saturdays, or explore the roadside stands and farms dotted along the [CultivATE Taste Trail](https://www.niagarabenchlands.ca/cultivate), which winds its way through the Niagara Benchlands.

## Action-Packed Autumn Adventures For Grade Schoolers

Niagara's fall festivals offer all kinds of fun for families with kids who love to get in on the action and try new things. September's [Niagara Grape & Wine Fall Festival](https://niagarawinefestival.com/) wraps up with a final day featuring special activities for kids that include a Pied Piper Parade where children are invited to dress up in costumes and join a spirited procession through Montebello Park. There's also a Telus Family Fun Zone with inflatable games, face painting, hands-on crafts, and a wide range of kid-friendly activities.

In early October, the [Cicada Music and Arts Festival](https://www.cicadafestival.ca/) is a family-friendly event that welcomes guests of all ages, with free admission for children ages 12 and under and discounted tickets for youth ages 12-17. Talk about a fun way to introduce the kids to incredible performers like Metric, Bahamas, Hawksley Workman, and more!

On Thanksgiving weekend, the [Ball's Falls Thanksgiving Festival](https://thanksgivingfestival.ca/) is a can't-miss event for families with a variety of kid-friendly activities including face painting, train rides, birds-of-prey demonstrations, magic shows, historical tours, and more.

Kids this age will also enjoy having a hands-on harvest experience, like:

* Picking your own pears at [Cherry Avenue Farms](https://www.cherryavenuefarms.org/) in September
* Choosing the perfect pumpkin for Halloween carving at [Romagnoli Farms'](https://www.romagnolifarms.com/) two Beamsville markets
* Taking the [Discover Maple Tour at White Meadows Farms](https://whitemeadowsfarms.com/discover-maple-tour/) for a sweet lesson in how maple syrup is made
* Participating in one of The Watering Can's [one-hour fall workshops](https://thewateringcan.ca/workshop/kids-fall-workshop) designed for children up to the age of 10.

## Screen-Free Fall Fun For Tweens and Teens

Finding engaging activities that will help the older kids forget all about their devices is easy in Niagara. Here are just a few ideas that the kids will never see coming:

* Enjoy a retro film together at [The Film House](https://www.firstontariopac.ca/Online/default.asp?BOparam::WScontent::loadArticle::permalink=FilmHouse&BOparam::WScontent::loadArticle::context_id=&menu_id=0BDE6C02-6088-4CF5-B51D-63651FF422DF), like 2001: A Space Odyssey or The Rocky Horror Picture Show.
* Get in the Halloween spirit on a [Haunted Walking Tour of historic Jordan Village](https://lincolnmuseum.ca/whats-on/event/haunted-walking-tour-jordan-village).
* [Rent an illuminated clear-bottom kayak](https://niagaraboatrentals.com/kayak-rental) and explore Jordan Harbour by moonlight.
* Go on a family hunt to track down Niagara's best buttertart by making stops at [13th Street Winery's Bakery](https://13thstreetwinery.com/discover/13th-street-bakery/), [Beamsville Bakery](https://www.beamsvillebakery.ca/products.html), [Tay's Treats](https://www.facebook.com/lovetaystreats/), and [Blossom Bakery](http://www.blossombakery.ca/).
