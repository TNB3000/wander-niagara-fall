Whether you like to rise with the sun or prefer to have your fun under the light of the autumn moon, fall in Niagara offers round-the-clock opportunities to get out and explore the region during one of the most beautiful times of the year. Use our insider's suggested daytime and evening itineraries to inspire your own weekend getaway to Niagara this fall.

## A Rustic and Fun Fall Day

Start your day with a small-town breakfast. [Penny's Kitchen Diner and Coffeehouse](https://pennyskitchen.ca/) in Vineland or the [Campden General Store](https://www.campdengeneralstore.com/) are both local favourites.

Next, get some fresh air on a morning hike. Choose between a [Niagara Benchlands](https://www.niagarabenchlands.ca/) wander at [Ball's Falls Conservation Area](https://npca.ca/parks-recreation/conservation-areas/balls-falls), which hosts its beloved [Thanksgiving Festival](https://thanksgivingfestival.ca/) in October, or a more urban stroll in [St. Catharines](https://lovestc.ca/). To get a better sense of St. Catharines' interesting past, take advantage of the [St. Catharines Museum's](https://www.stcatharines.ca/arts-culture-and-events/st-catharines-museum-and-welland-canals-centre/) [History Insight installations](https://stcatharinesmuseumblog.com/history-insite/), which set historic photos in modern streetscapes to provide fascinating context for the important moments that once unfolded in or near those locations. For another fun way to get your steps in while learning about the city, take one of the daytime walking tours offered this fall showcasing the [Welland Canal](https://events.stcatharines.ca/museum/Detail/2026-10-03-0900-Walking-Tour-Merritt-Trail-Hike-the-Canal), [Yates Street heritage district](https://events.stcatharines.ca/museum/Detail/2026-10-17-1000-Walking-Tour-Yates-Street-Heritage-Architecture), [downtown churches](https://events.stcatharines.ca/museum/Detail/2026-10-24-1000-Walking-Tour-Downtown-Churches-of-Church-Street), [Port Dalhousie](https://events.stcatharines.ca/museum/Detail/2026-11-28-1000-Walking-Tours-Port-Dalhousie-Village-and-Villagers), [Victoria Lawn Cemetery](https://events.stcatharines.ca/museum/Detail/2026-11-21-1000-Walking-Tours-Victoria-Lawn-Cemetery), and [Western Hill](https://events.stcatharines.ca/museum/Detail/2026-11-14-1000-Walking-Tours-Western-Hill-Whats-Brewing-in-Wester).

Fuel up after your morning walk with lunch and a tasting flight at a local craft brewery. If you've hiked Ball's Falls, [Bench Brewing Company](https://www.benchbrewing.com/), [Newark Brewing](https://www.newarkbrewing.com/), or the just-opened [The Brook Beer Garden](https://thebrookbrewing.com/) are all fantastic options. If you're in St. Catharines, opt for [Cold Break Brewing](https://coldbreak.ca/), [The Merchant Ale House](https://merchantalehouse.com/), or [Decew Falls Brewing Co.](https://decewfallsbrewing.ca/)

After lunch, lean into harvest season by visiting a few of Niagara's wineries that are located in beautiful barns, such as [Peninsula Ridge Estates Winery](https://peninsularidge.com/), [Villa Romana Estate Winery](https://www.villaromana.ca/), and [Honsberger Estate Winery](https://www.honsbergerestate.com/). Be sure to check the [Niagara Benchlands Wineries](https://niagarabenchlandswineries.com/pages/events) event calendar before your trip to find special events being hosted at wineries while you're here. Other fun options for a fall afternoon include apple picking at [Silmaril Farms](http://silmarilfarm.ca/), hunting for vintage treasures at [Prudhomme's Market](https://www.instagram.com/prudhommesantiques/?hl=en), sipping your way through a cocktail-making class at [Dillon's Small Batch Distillers](https://www.dillons.ca/), or taking a workshop to create a signature fall urn, wreath, or table arrangement at [The Watering Can](https://thewateringcan.ca/).

Enjoy an early dinner at the [Redstone Winery Restaurant](https://www.instagram.com/redstonewinery) in Beamsville. You'll experience a culinary celebration of all Niagara has to offer, with seasonally inspired menus using fresh, locally grown ingredients.

## An Action-Packed Autumn Evening

Start your evening adventure early with a deliciously dark and dramatic late-afternoon wine tasting. [Henry of Pelham Family Estate](https://henryofpelham.com/) offers a [Candlelit Cellar Tasting](https://www.instagram.com/p/DY7TsDhBKBU/) set within Canada's largest underground working barrel cellar.

Next, make your way to [Downtown St. Catharines](https://mydowntown.ca/) for a happy hour cocktail or mocktail at [Wandering Spirits](https://www.wanderingspirits.ca/) or [Siris Tasting Room](https://siristastingroom.ca/). If you're visiting in September, consider kicking off spooky season with a [Guided Spirit Walk at Victoria Lawn Cemetery](https://www.facebook.com/stcatharinesmuseum/posts/announcing-the-15th-annual-guided-spirit-walks-at-victoria-lawn-cemetery-st-cath/1670235998443615/), where costumed guides will act out theatrical scenes from the city's past throughout the cemetery.

Enjoy dinner at one of St. Catharines' many excellent restaurants. [Restaurant Capelin](https://www.restaurantcapelin.com/) is an exciting new option, offering a fish-focused menu that prioritizes freshwater species from the cold, clear waters of the Great Lakes and Georgian Bay. Squeezing in one last al fresco dinner on the rooftop at [Solaia Cucina & Cantina](https://solaia.ca/) is another fun option.

After dinner, catch a screening of a unique documentary, new feature, or classic film with a glass of Niagara wine or craft beer at [The Film House](https://firstontariopac.ca/Online/default.asp?BOparam::WScontent::loadArticle::permalink=FilmHouse&BOparam::WScontent::loadArticle::context_id=). You can also check the autumn calendars at [Warehouse Concert Hall](https://warehouseconcerthall.com/), [FirstOntario Performing Arts Centre](https://www.firstontariopac.ca/Online/default.asp), and [Helliwell Hall](https://helliwellhall.com/) if you'd prefer an evening spent enjoying live music.

Afterward, [Twenty Kitchen](https://twentyrestaurant.ca/), right across the street, is a perfect place to enjoy a late-night snack and a tasty cocktail at the bar.

## Find The Perfect Place To Overnight

Whether you're calling it a day right after dinner or keeping the party going late into the night, having a lovely place to lay your head is a must. Those who prefer to overnight in the Niagara Benchlands can opt for a cozy [vineyard casetta at Al Borgo Estates](https://alborgoestates.com/the-casetta) or an orchard cabin at [Inn The Orchard](https://inntheorchard.com/). For a stay that's close to all the action of downtown St. Catharines, consider [Springbank House](https://www.springbankhouse.ca/), the [Stone Mill Inn](https://www.stonemillinn.ca/), or the [Holiday Inn and Suites St. Catharines](https://www.ihg.com/holidayinn/hotels/us/en/st-catharines/ycmsa/hoteldetail).
