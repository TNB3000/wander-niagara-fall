Fall is the best time to visit the bench. The vines turn gold and red. The air is cool. And the wineries are calm after the summer rush.

Here is an easy two-day plan for a couples' or friends' getaway.

## Day one: wine and a long lunch

Start on the bench, below the escarpment. Book two or three wineries and take your time. Most have a patio and a fire.

![A fall vineyard on the escarpment bench](/assets/img/placeholder-inline.svg)
*[PLACEHOLDER image — the client is supplying real photos.]*

Have a long lunch between tastings. Then check in to a small inn with a view of the vines.

## Day two: colour and a nice dinner

Take a short morning walk to see the fall colours. Grab a coffee in downtown St. Catharines. Then pick one more winery or a cidery before you head home.

End with an early dinner at a chef's table. Book ahead — fall weekends fill up fast.

That's it. Two easy days, no rush, all on the bench.
