Fall is harvest time on the bench. The markets fill up with pumpkins, apples and fresh food. Here's how to make the most of a market day.

## Go on a Saturday

Most markets are busiest and best on Saturday mornings. That's when you'll find the most stalls and the freshest picks.

![A harvest market stall in fall](/assets/img/placeholder-inline.svg)
*[PLACEHOLDER image — real photos coming from the client.]*

## Bring cash and a bag

Some stalls take cards, but cash is faster. Bring a tote or a box for everything you'll carry home.

## Make it a day

Pair the market with a farm visit. Pick apples, walk a corn maze, or let the kids meet the animals. Then head home with a trunk full of fall.

Simple, fresh and fun. That's a harvest market day on the bench.
