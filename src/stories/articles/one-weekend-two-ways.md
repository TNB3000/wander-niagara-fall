Same region, two moods. Wander Niagara one weekend and choose your speed: rustic charm in the Benchlands, or upscale elegance in St. Catharines. Or stay over and do both.

## Rustic charm in the Niagara Benchlands

Start with coffee and breakfast at the Campden General Store. Then hike Ball's Falls — and if it's Thanksgiving weekend, you'll land in the middle of the festival.

![The general store porch, pumpkins out front](/assets/img/photos/inline-general-store.jpg)

Lunch is a beer flight at Newark Brewery or the new Brook Brewing. Spend the afternoon touring Niagara's most beautiful barn-based wineries — Peninsula Ridge, Villa Romana, Calamus and Honsberger.

Dinner is exactly what it sounds like at Just Cooking Rustic Italian. Stay the night at the new Al Borgo Estates casettas. In the morning, breakfast at Penny's Diner, then go apple picking or vintage-treasure hunting at Prudhomme's.

## Upscale elegance in St. Catharines

Start late afternoon with a candlelit cellar tasting in Canada's largest underground working barrel cellar at Henry of Pelham.

Dinner is the tasting menu at Restaurant Capelin — or a last alfresco dinner of the season on the rooftop at Solaia.

Then an evening concert at the PAC. If you time it right, the Spark Gala on October 24 features the National Ballet of Canada.

## Why not both?

Add an overnight at the new Al Borgo Estates casettas and run the rustic day into the elegant one. One weekend, two ways, no wrong answer.
