You don't need a big hike to see fall colours with kids. The bench has lots of short, flat trails. Here are a few easy ones.

## Pick a short loop

Look for trails under an hour. Flat paths are best for little legs and strollers. Bring water and a snack.

![A leafy fall trail on the bench](/assets/img/placeholder-inline.svg)
*[PLACEHOLDER image — real photos coming from the client.]*

## Add a treat

Most walks are near a farm or a market. Finish with cider donuts or a hot apple cider. It makes the day feel special.

## Go early

Weekends get busy in October. Go in the morning to find parking and quiet trails.

Short walk, big colours, happy kids. That's a good fall day.
