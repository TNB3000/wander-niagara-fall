Fall here works for every age. Here's the plan, stage by stage.

## Babies

Take a fall stroller ride on the Welland Canal Trail. Or bring baby along in a sling for an easygoing walk on the trails at Calamus Estate Winery.

## Toddlers

Ride the Port Dalhousie carousel and visit Happy Rolph's — both run until Thanksgiving weekend. Then discover new favourite foods at the area's markets and farm stands.

## Grade schoolers

This is festival season: Grape & Wine Family Day and the Pied Piper Parade, Fling on King, the Ball's Falls Thanksgiving Festival, and Cicada Music Festival. Add apple and pumpkin picking, and the Discover Maple tour at White Meadows.

![Into the corn maze](/assets/img/photos/inline-corn-maze.jpg)

## Tweens

Fall kids' workshops at The Watering Can, Cinderella at the PAC, and a tour through Morningstar Mill.

## Teens

A haunted walking tour of Jordan Village, or an autumn paddle on Jordan Harbour. Cooler than it sounds — tell them it's neither.

![The barrel train, for the littlest wanderers](/assets/img/photos/inline-barrel-train.jpg)

Whatever the age, pack layers. The bench does all four seasons in one October afternoon.
